# Delete your Bolix account and data

**App:** Bolix  **Developer:** BOLIX MCHJ  **Contact:** info@bolix.uz

[O'zbekcha versiya pastda / Uzbek version below](#bolix-akkaunti-va-ma'lumotlarini-o'chirish)

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **info@bolix.uz** from the email address linked to your account, or write from the phone number you use to sign in. Use the subject **"Delete my Bolix account"**.
2. Include: the phone number of your account, your role (parent), and whether you want to delete the **whole family** (including the child profiles) or only **some data** (for example location history).
3. We may ask you to confirm your identity (for example with a one-time code sent to your phone number).
4. We complete the deletion within **30 days** and confirm by email.

A parent can request deletion of their child's data in the same way. Removing the child's device from the family in the app also stops all further data collection from that device.

## What is deleted

- Account profile: name, phone number, avatar, language settings.
- Child profiles: name, age or date of birth, avatar.
- Location history and geofences.
- Device status data and push notification tokens.
- Chat messages and shared photos or voice messages.
- Task data and task proof media.
- App rules, modes and screen time statistics.

## What may be kept

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Consent and audit records | 5 years, required by law and for audit |
| Device integrity checks | up to 90 days |
| Data that we must keep to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes | for the period required by law |

After this period the data is deleted or anonymized. Backups are overwritten within 30 days after deletion.

## Delete only some data

You can ask to delete only part of your data (for example location history or chat messages) without deleting your account. Email **info@bolix.uz** and list what you want deleted.

# Bolix akkaunti va ma'lumotlarini o'chirish

**Ilova:** Bolix  **Dasturchi:** BOLIX MChJ  **Aloqa:** info@bolix.uz

## O'chirishni qanday so'rash mumkin

1. Akkauntingizga bog'langan emaildan yoki kirishda ishlatadigan telefon raqamingizdan **info@bolix.uz** ga xat yuboring. Mavzu: **"Bolix akkauntimni o'chiring"**.
2. Xatda quyidagilarni yozing: akkauntning telefon raqami, rolingiz (ota-ona) va **butun oilani** (bola profillari bilan) yoki faqat **ba'zi ma'lumotlarni** (masalan, joylashuv tarixini) o'chirishni xohlashingiz.
3. Shaxsingizni tasdiqlashni so'rashimiz mumkin (masalan, telefon raqamingizga yuborilgan bir martalik kod bilan).
4. O'chirishni **30 kun** ichida bajaramiz va email orqali xabar beramiz.

Ota-ona bolasining ma'lumotini ham xuddi shunday o'chirishni so'rashi mumkin. Ilovada bola qurilmasini oiladan olib tashlash ham shu qurilmadan keyingi ma'lumot yig'ilishini to'xtatadi.

## Nima o'chiriladi

- Akkaunt profili: ism, telefon raqami, avatar, til sozlamalari.
- Bola profillari: ism, yosh yoki tug'ilgan sana, avatar.
- Joylashuv tarixi va geozonalar.
- Qurilma holati ma'lumotlari va push-tokenlar.
- Chat xabarlari, yuborilgan rasm va ovozli xabarlar.
- Vazifalar va ularning isbot mediasi.
- Ilova qoidalari, rejimlar va ekran vaqti statistikasi.

## Nima saqlanib qolishi mumkin

| Ma'lumot | Saqlash muddati |
|---|---|
| Rozilik va audit yozuvlari | 5 yil (qonun va audit talabi) |
| Qurilma yaxlitligi tekshiruvlari | 90 kungacha |
| Qonuniy majburiyatlar yoki nizolarni hal qilish uchun saqlashimiz shart bo'lgan ma'lumot | Qonun talab qilgan muddat |

Bu muddatdan keyin ma'lumot o'chiriladi yoki anonim qilinadi. Zaxira nusxalar o'chirishdan keyin 30 kun ichida ustidan yoziladi.

## Faqat ba'zi ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Akkauntni o'chirmasdan, ma'lumotning bir qismini (masalan, joylashuv tarixi yoki chat xabarlarini) o'chirishni so'rashingiz mumkin. **info@bolix.uz** ga yozing va nimani o'chirish kerakligini ko'rsating.
