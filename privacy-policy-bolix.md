# Privacy Policy — Bolix

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

[O'zbekcha versiya pastda / Uzbek version below](#maxfiylik-siyosati--bolix)

Bolix ("the App", "we", "us") is a parental control and family safety application operated by **BOLIX MCHJ** (a limited liability company registered in the Republic of Uzbekistan). This policy explains what data the App collects, why, how it is protected, and what choices you have.

Contact: **info@bolix.uz**

---

## 1. How the App works

The same App is installed on two kinds of devices:

- **Parent device** — the parent or guardian creates a family account, connects a child's device, and sets rules (modes, geofences, tasks, allowed apps).
- **Child device** — the child uses the App with the parent's knowledge. The child's device sends status information to the parent.

The App is intended for **families**. The Child mode is meant to be set up by a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly allow a child to create an account without a parent. A parent who connects a child's device confirms that they are the child's parent or legal guardian and consent to the data collection described below on the child's behalf.

## 2. Data we collect

### 2.1 Account and family data
- Name or nickname, phone number (used to sign in), role (parent / child), family membership.
- Child's profile: display name, age or date of birth, avatar.
- Language preference.

### 2.2 Location data (Child device)
- **Precise location (GPS)** to show the child's position to the parent and to trigger geofence alerts (for example "arrived at school").
- **Background location**: location is collected also when the App is closed or not in use, because the parent needs to see where the child is during the day. A persistent notification is shown on the child's device while this runs.
- Location history is kept for **12 months**.

### 2.3 Device status
- Battery level, network status, whether location and required permissions are enabled, App version, device model and operating system version.
- Alerts if protection is disabled or the App is removed.
- Device integrity signal (Google Play Integrity) to detect tampering. Results are kept for 90 days.

### 2.4 Rules and app control
- Which apps are allowed in each mode set by the parent (for example study or bedtime mode), screen time statistics for apps, and requests the child sends to the parent.
- To show a friendly screen instead of a blocked app, the App may use Android's **Accessibility Service** and, where enabled, a local **VPN service** for web filtering. These are used **only** to detect which app is in the foreground and to apply the parent's rules. They do **not** read screen content, keystrokes, messages, passwords or the content of web pages. Web filtering runs on the device; browsing content is not sent to us.

### 2.5 Safety features
- **SOS**: when the child presses SOS, we send the child's current location and the alert to the parent and may play a loud sound on the child's device.
- **Tasks**: tasks created by parents and their completion status. Where a task needs a photo, video or voice proof, that media is stored for **30 days**.

### 2.6 Messages
- Family chat and support chat messages. Chat messages are **encrypted on our servers** and stored for **90 days**.
- Voice messages (microphone) and photos (camera or gallery) only when you choose to send them.

### 2.7 Notifications and technical data
- A push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) to deliver alerts.
- Crash and diagnostic data and IP address needed to run and secure the service.

### 2.8 Consent records
- We keep a log of consents (for example acceptance of this policy and of sensitive permissions) for **5 years**, as a legal and audit record.

We do **not** collect: contacts, SMS, call logs, the list of other installed apps beyond what is needed for app rules, or financial information. We do **not** sell personal data and do **not** use it for advertising profiles of children.

## 3. Permissions and why we need them

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Location (precise, coarse, background) | Show child's location to the parent, geofence alerts, SOS |
| Foreground service (location, data sync) | Keep location sharing and sync working reliably |
| Camera | Scan the QR code to connect devices; take task proof photos |
| Microphone | Voice messages in chat |
| Notifications, full-screen notifications | Deliver alerts, SOS and loud-call alerts to the parent |
| Internet / network state | Communicate with our servers |
| Usage access | Screen time and app usage statistics |
| Ignore battery optimization, boot completed, wake lock | Keep protection running after the phone restarts or in power saving mode |
| Accessibility Service | Detect the foreground app to apply parent's app rules (see 2.4) |
| VPN service | On-device web filtering (see 2.4) |

Each sensitive permission is explained in the App before Android asks for it. You can withdraw permissions in the device settings at any time; some features will then stop working.

## 4. How we use the data

- To provide the features above (location, rules, SOS, tasks, chat, support).
- To keep the service secure and detect abuse or tampering.
- To fix bugs and improve the App.
- To meet legal obligations.

We do not use data for third-party advertising.

## 5. Sharing

Data is visible to the **members of the same family** according to their role (parents see the child's data; a child cannot see other families' data).

We use service providers who process data on our behalf, only to run the App:
- **Google Firebase** (push notifications) and **Google Play Integrity** (device integrity),
- **Google ML Kit** (QR code scanning, processed on the device),
- **Yandex MapKit** (map display),
- hosting and storage providers for our servers.

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect someone's safety (for example in an emergency). We do not sell data.

## 6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Chat messages and sensitive data are encrypted on our servers, and access is limited to authorized staff. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Retention and deletion

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Location history | 12 months |
| Chat messages | 90 days |
| Task proof media | 30 days |
| Device integrity checks | 90 days |
| Consent and audit records | 5 years |
| Account and family data | While the account exists |

**To delete your account and data**, email **info@bolix.uz** from the phone number or email used for sign-in, or use the delete option in the App if available. We will delete or anonymize personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law. A parent can also request deletion of their child's data. Removing the child's device from the family stops further collection.

## 8. Children's privacy

The Child mode is used by children only with a parent or guardian who sets it up and controls it. Parents can review, request correction of, or request deletion of their child's data at any time by contacting **info@bolix.uz**. We do not ask a child for more data than is needed for the features above, and we do not show targeted advertising to children.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent, by contacting **info@bolix.uz**. We process requests in accordance with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data".

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, and we will notify users in the App about important changes.

## 11. Contact

Email: **info@bolix.uz**

# Maxfiylik siyosati — Bolix

**Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 19-noyabr**

Bolix ("Ilova", "biz") — **BOLIX MChJ** (O'zbekiston Respublikasida ro'yxatdan o'tgan mas'uliyati cheklangan jamiyat) tomonidan taqdim etiladigan ota-onalar nazorati va oilaviy xavfsizlik ilovasi. Ushbu siyosat Ilova qanday ma'lumot yig'ishi, nima uchun, qanday himoyalanishi va sizda qanday tanlov borligini tushuntiradi.

Aloqa: **info@bolix.uz**

## 1. Ilova qanday ishlaydi

Bitta Ilova ikki xil qurilmada o'rnatiladi:

- **Ota-ona qurilmasi** — ota-ona yoki vasiy oila akkauntini yaratadi, bolaning qurilmasini ulaydi va qoidalarni (rejimlar, geozonalar, vazifalar, ruxsat etilgan ilovalar) belgilaydi.
- **Bola qurilmasi** — bola Ilovadan ota-onasining xabardorligida foydalanadi. Qurilma holati haqidagi ma'lumot ota-onaga yuboriladi.

Ilova **oilalar** uchun mo'ljallangan. Bola rejimini ota-ona yoki qonuniy vasiy sozlashi kerak. Ota-onasiz bola akkaunt yaratishiga ataylab yo'l qo'ymaymiz. Bolaning qurilmasini ulagan ota-ona o'zini bolaning ota-onasi yoki qonuniy vasiysi deb tasdiqlaydi va quyida yozilgan ma'lumotlarni yig'ishga bola nomidan rozilik beradi.

## 2. Qanday ma'lumot yig'amiz

**Akkaunt va oila:** ism yoki taxallus, telefon raqami (kirish uchun), rol (ota-ona / bola), oila a'zoligi; bolaning profili (ko'rsatiladigan ism, yoshi yoki tug'ilgan sanasi, avatar); til sozlamasi.

**Joylashuv (bola qurilmasi):**
- Ota-onaga bolaning o'rnini ko'rsatish va geozona xabarlarini ("maktabga yetib keldi") yuborish uchun **aniq joylashuv (GPS)**.
- **Fon rejimidagi joylashuv**: Ilova yopiq yoki ishlatilmayotgan paytda ham joylashuv yig'iladi, chunki ota-ona bolaning kun davomida qayerdaligini ko'rishi kerak. Bu ishlayotganda bola qurilmasida doimiy bildirishnoma ko'rinadi.
- Joylashuv tarixi **12 oy** saqlanadi.

**Qurilma holati:** batareya darajasi, tarmoq holati, joylashuv va kerakli ruxsatlar yoqilganligi, Ilova versiyasi, qurilma modeli va tizim versiyasi; himoya o'chirilsa yoki Ilova o'chirib tashlansa ogohlantirish; qurilma yaxlitligini tekshirish (Google Play Integrity), natijalar 90 kun saqlanadi.

**Qoidalar va ilovalarni boshqarish:** ota-ona belgilagan har bir rejimda (masalan, dars yoki uyqu vaqti) ruxsat etilgan ilovalar, ilovalar bo'yicha ekran vaqti statistikasi, bolaning ota-onaga yuborgan so'rovlari. Bloklangan ilova o'rniga do'stona ekran ko'rsatish uchun Ilova Android **Maxsus imkoniyatlar xizmati (Accessibility Service)** va yoqilgan bo'lsa, veb-filtr uchun qurilmaning o'zidagi **VPN xizmati**dan foydalanishi mumkin. Ular **faqat** qaysi ilova ochiq ekanini aniqlash va ota-ona qoidalarini qo'llash uchun ishlatiladi. Ular ekran mazmuni, bosilgan tugmalar, xabarlar, parollar yoki veb-sahifa mazmunini **o'qimaydi**. Veb-filtr qurilmada ishlaydi, ko'rilgan sahifalar mazmuni bizga yuborilmaydi.

**Xavfsizlik funksiyalari:**
- **SOS**: bola SOS ni bossa, uning joriy joylashuvi va signal ota-onaga yuboriladi, bola qurilmasida baland ovoz chalinishi mumkin.
- **Vazifalar**: ota-ona yaratgan vazifalar va ularning bajarilish holati. Vazifada foto, video yoki ovozli isbot kerak bo'lsa, bu media **30 kun** saqlanadi.

**Xabarlar:** oilaviy chat va qo'llab-quvvatlash chati xabarlari. Chat xabarlari **serverlarimizda shifrlangan** holda **90 kun** saqlanadi. Ovozli xabar (mikrofon) va rasm (kamera yoki galereya) faqat siz yuborishni tanlaganingizda ishlatiladi.

**Bildirishnoma va texnik ma'lumotlar:** signallarni yetkazish uchun push-token (Firebase Cloud Messaging); xizmatni ishlatish va himoyalash uchun zarur bo'lgan xatolik/diagnostika ma'lumotlari va IP-manzil.

**Rozilik yozuvlari:** rozilik qayd jurnali (masalan, siyosatni va sezgir ruxsatlarni qabul qilish) huquqiy va audit yozuvi sifatida **5 yil** saqlanadi.

Biz **yig'maymiz**: kontaktlar, SMS, qo'ng'iroqlar tarixi, ilova qoidalari uchun kerak bo'lganidan ortiq o'rnatilgan ilovalar ro'yxati, moliyaviy ma'lumotlar. Shaxsiy ma'lumotlarni **sotmaymiz** va bolalar uchun reklama profillari yaratmaymiz.

## 4. Ma'lumotdan qanday foydalanamiz

Yuqoridagi funksiyalarni (joylashuv, qoidalar, SOS, vazifalar, chat, qo'llab-quvvatlash) ta'minlash; xizmat xavfsizligini saqlash va suiiste'mol yoki buzishni aniqlash; xatolarni tuzatish va Ilovani yaxshilash; qonuniy talablarni bajarish. Ma'lumotni uchinchi tomon reklamasi uchun ishlatmaymiz.

## 5. Ma'lumot kimga ko'rinadi

Ma'lumot **bir oila a'zolariga** roliga qarab ko'rinadi (ota-ona bolaning ma'lumotini ko'radi; boshqa oilalarning ma'lumoti ko'rinmaydi).

Biz nomimizdan ma'lumotni faqat Ilova ishlashi uchun qayta ishlaydigan xizmat ko'rsatuvchilardan foydalanamiz: **Google Firebase** (push-bildirishnoma), **Google Play Integrity** (qurilma yaxlitligi), **Google ML Kit** (QR skanerlash, qurilmada), **Yandex MapKit** (xarita), server hosting va saqlash provayderlari. Qonun talab qilsa yoki kimningdir xavfsizligini himoya qilish uchun (masalan, favqulodda holatda) ma'lumotni oshkor qilishimiz mumkin. Ma'lumotni sotmaymiz.

## 6. Xavfsizlik

Ma'lumot HTTPS orqali uzatiladi. Chat xabarlari va sezgir ma'lumotlar serverlarda shifrlanadi, kirish vakolatli xodimlar bilan cheklangan. Hech bir tizim to'liq xavfsiz emas, shuning uchun mutlaq xavfsizlikni kafolatlay olmaymiz.
